# Contributing Scam and Fraud Reports

Contributions should help establish verifiable facts rather than amplify accusations.

## Useful submissions

Include, where available: marketplace/platform, listing URL, archived URL, date observed, item or service, asking price, seller identity as presented, requested payment method, screenshots or source material, relevant communications, proof of copied text/images, platform status notices, and a concise explanation of the suspected fraud indicators.

## Privacy

Do not publish passwords, authentication codes, full payment credentials, bank information, private financial account numbers, government identification numbers, home addresses that are not already materially relevant public business information, or other unnecessary sensitive data.

Personal identifiers should be published only when necessary to understand a documented public-facing scam indicator and should be redacted when the public-interest value does not justify disclosure.

## Writing standard

Prefer: “The seller account presented the name X and requested payment by Y.”

Avoid: “X is a scammer,” unless verified identity and evidence independently justify that statement under the project's Confirmed Fraud standard.

## Classification

Contributors may suggest a classification, but publication should follow the definitions in `METHODOLOGY.md`: Confirmed Fraud, Likely Scam, High Risk, Unverified, or Cleared / Resolved.
