# Copied Listing Scams

A copied-listing scam reuses photographs, descriptions, specifications, or other sale material from a legitimate earlier advertisement to make a fraudulent offer appear authentic.

Strong evidence can include an independently dated original listing, distinctive text reproduced substantially or verbatim, photographs traceable to an earlier seller, or an item shown as sold before the later advertisement appeared.

Copied material is particularly significant when combined with an unusually low price, unprotected payment request, inability to produce current proof of possession, or account deactivation.
