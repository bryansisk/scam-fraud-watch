# Marketplace Scams and Fraudulent Online Listings

Marketplace fraud can involve nonexistent merchandise, copied listings, hijacked accounts, impersonated sellers, counterfeit goods, false shipping claims, or attempts to move payment outside a platform's protected checkout system.

High-risk indicators can include a price materially below the normal market without a credible explanation, copied photographs or text, a newly created or deactivated account, refusal to provide current proof of possession, inconsistent seller history, urgency, and demands for irreversible or unprotected payment.

No single indicator proves fraud. SCAM Watch evaluates the combined evidence and assigns a classification under the project methodology.
