# Impersonation Scams and Stolen Seller Identities

Fraud accounts may use a real person's name, photographs, business identity, email-like identifier, or other biographical details without that person's knowledge.

For that reason, SCAM Watch treats account identity and verified real-world identity as separate questions. Unless independently established, case reports describe a **seller identity as presented** rather than attributing conduct to the real person whose name or image appears.

This distinction helps warn consumers about the account behavior without incorrectly accusing an impersonated person.
