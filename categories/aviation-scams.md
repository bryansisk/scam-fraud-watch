# Aviation Marketplace Scams

Aviation equipment can attract marketplace fraud because high-value headsets, avionics, aircraft parts, engines, instruments, and accessories are specialized products with substantial resale value.

Buyers should verify the exact model and configuration, current proof of possession, serial information where appropriate, seller history, listing originality, and a protected payment path. For modular products, confirm that the advertisement includes every component required for a complete usable unit rather than only a replacement module or subassembly.

SCAM Watch aviation cases apply the same evidence and identity standards used throughout the repository.
