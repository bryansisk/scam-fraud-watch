# Payment Scams and Unprotected Payment Requests

Fraudulent sellers frequently attempt to shift buyers from protected marketplace checkout or commercial payment methods to payment channels that provide little or no merchandise-purchase protection.

For a remote purchase from an unknown private seller, requests for person-to-person transfers, wire transfers, cryptocurrency, gift cards, or other difficult-to-reverse payment methods should materially increase scrutiny. The risk is especially high when combined with copied listing content, unverifiable possession, identity inconsistencies, or pressure to pay quickly.

A payment method alone does not establish fraud. Classification depends on the complete evidentiary record.
