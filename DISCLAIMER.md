# Disclaimer

SCAM Watch is an informational, evidence-focused consumer fraud research project. It is not a law-enforcement agency, court, credit bureau, identity-verification service, or legal advisor.

A published account name or seller identity may be false, stolen, spoofed, or impersonated. Unless expressly documented as verified, references to a seller identity describe the identity **presented by the account or listing**, not a determination that a particular real person controlled the account.

Classifications reflect the evidence available at the stated review date and may change as new information becomes available. Readers should independently verify material facts before making financial, legal, or safety decisions.

Nothing in this repository guarantees that an unlisted seller or transaction is safe, or that every listed warning ultimately proves fraudulent. Corrections supported by reliable evidence are encouraged and should be handled under `CORRECTIONS.md`.
