# SCAM Watch

SCAM Watch documents evidence-backed online scam and fraud indicators so consumers can recognize risky listings, payment demands, impersonation, copied advertisements, and recurring fraud patterns.

## Classification labels

**Confirmed Fraud** · **Likely Scam** · **High Risk** · **Unverified** · **Cleared / Resolved**

A displayed seller name is not automatically the verified identity of the person controlling an account. Read the [methodology](METHODOLOGY.md), [disclaimer](DISCLAIMER.md), and [correction policy](CORRECTIONS.md) before interpreting a case.
