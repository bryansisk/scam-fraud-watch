# SCAM Watch

SCAM Watch is an evidence-focused consumer research project. It explains warning signs in online listings and payment requests, and is designed to publish traceable incident reports only after an evidence review. **A warning sign is not proof that a seller committed fraud.**

**Publication status:** No evidence-reviewed individual incident reports have been published in the public dataset yet. The category guides below describe fraud patterns and precautions, not findings against any named person or business.

## Explore scam and fraud warning signs

- [Marketplace listing scams]({{ '/categories/marketplace-scams.html' | relative_url }}) — check possession claims, seller history, listing originality, and payment conditions.
- [Payment scams and unprotected transfers]({{ '/categories/payment-scams.html' | relative_url }}) — understand payment risks without assuming the payment method proves fraud.
- [Copied-listing scams]({{ '/categories/copied-listing-scams.html' | relative_url }}) — evaluate reused photos, descriptions, and independently dated advertisements.
- [Seller impersonation scams]({{ '/categories/impersonation-scams.html' | relative_url }}) — distinguish an account's displayed identity from a verified person.
- [Aviation equipment scams]({{ '/categories/aviation-scams.html' | relative_url }}) — research warning signs involving headsets, avionics, aircraft parts, and related listings.

## How SCAM Watch classifies evidence

**Confirmed Fraud** · **Likely Scam** · **High Risk** · **Unverified** · **Cleared / Resolved**

These labels reflect the available evidence, not a presumption of guilt. A displayed name, photograph, or payment handle is not proof of who controlled an account. See the [classification methodology]({{ '/METHODOLOGY.html' | relative_url }}), [disclaimer]({{ '/DISCLAIMER.html' | relative_url }}), and [corrections policy]({{ '/CORRECTIONS.html' | relative_url }}).

## Contribute verifiable evidence

Before suggesting a report, read the [submission and redaction guidelines]({{ '/CONTRIBUTING.html' | relative_url }}). Do not publicly post sensitive financial data, private addresses, or unverified personal identities.
