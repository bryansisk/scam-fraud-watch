# Bose A30 aviation headset — AirplaneFlyers copied-listing and payment dispute

**SCAM Watch incident:** SW-2026-0001  
**Classification:** Likely Scam (evidence-based consumer warning; not a legal finding)  
**Reviewed:** 2026-11-09  
**Marketplace:** AirplaneFlyers  
**Item:** Bose A30 aviation headset, Bluetooth, dual GA plugs  
**Advertised asking price:** $800  
**Buyer-reported agreed payment:** $700  
**Displayed seller identity:** “John Kirby,” Seattle, Washington — **not independently identity-verified**  
**Buyer-reported outcome:** Item not received as expected; American Express reversed the $700 card charge after dispute. The reversal is reported by the buyer, not independently documented by a public issuer record.

## Summary

A buyer reports agreeing to buy a Bose A30 Bluetooth dual-plug aviation headset advertised on AirplaneFlyers for $700, paying through PayPal Friends & Family with an American Express-funded transaction in late August 2026. The buyer reports that shipping proof did not arrive despite follow-up messages and that American Express subsequently reversed the card charge.

An independently accessible AirplaneFlyers advertisement titled **“Bose A30 Aviation Headset With Bluetooth”** shows an $800 asking price and a displayed seller name and city. Its product description closely tracks a **September 19, 2023** Reddit r/TheHangar listing that was publicly marked **sold by October 2, 2023**. The overlap includes claims about ten flight hours, keeping the headset in its Bose case, original packaging/accessories, unregistered warranty, and a nonsmoking home.

The reused wording is a meaningful warning sign, **not by itself proof** that the advertised item was unavailable or that the displayed name belongs to the account operator. The buyer's reported non-delivery/payment dispute strengthens the concern. The public record does not independently establish the legal identity or intent of the person who received the payment.

## Timeline and evidence

- **2023-09-19:** Reddit r/TheHangar Bose A30 Bluetooth dual-plug advertisement published, initially asking $1,025 shipped using PayPal Goods & Services.
- **2023-10-02:** Original Reddit advertisement marked SOLD.
- **2026-08-27–28:** Buyer reports negotiating the AirplaneFlyers advertisement to $700 and sending payment using PayPal Friends & Family, funded by American Express.
- **Late August–early September 2026:** Buyer reports requesting shipping/tracking details without receiving satisfactory proof of shipment.
- **By 2026-11-09:** Buyer confirms American Express reversed the $700 charge. The exact reversal date and issuer determination have not been independently verified.

## Warning indicators

- Substantial reuse of a distinctive sales description from an older advertisement already marked sold.
- Buyer-reported insistence on or use of an unprotected personal-payment channel for a merchandise purchase.
- Buyer-reported failure to supply satisfactory shipment evidence following payment.
- Subsequent buyer-reported American Express reversal of the $700 charge.

## Important identity and outcome limitations

“John Kirby” is the **name presented in the listing**, not an independently authenticated identity. The name could belong to an unrelated person or have been misused. This report does **not** accuse a verified individual of theft or criminal conduct, and does not assert a court finding. No personal email address, payment account, shipping address, or private financial record is published.

An American Express reversal documents the **buyer-reported financial outcome**, not by itself an adjudication that a particular person committed fraud. SCAM Watch will correct, downgrade, or clear the case if credible contradictory evidence becomes available.

## Consumer protection

Before paying for a high-value aviation headset, request a current dated proof-of-possession photo/video, verify the exact headset/control-module/plug configuration and serial number as appropriate, compare descriptions and photos with older listings, and use a purchase-protected payment method. Do not treat PayPal Friends & Family as equivalent to PayPal Goods & Services.
